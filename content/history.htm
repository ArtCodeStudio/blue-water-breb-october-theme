BREB GmbH & Co. KG (BREB) Cuxhaven, arose from Bremer Reederei Eilemann & Bischoff GmbH, established in Bremen in 1951 and managed by Master Mariner Captain Arne Ehlers and his team.

Since 2014, the company is based in Cuxhaven, the German Offshore-Industry-Center, with offices in Bremen, Sassnitz and Zeebrugge (BREB Belgium).

BREB is a traditional shipping company with own vessels, own chartering department and own port agency and a modern approach to clients and business.

Around 70% of the fleet sail under German flag. From 2003 to 2008, three environmental friendly vessels were built, according to the rules and regulations of “The Blue Angel”. BREB´s modern, geared multi-purpose vessels sail all European Waters from the White Sea in the North to the Black Sea in the South with special focus on their two North African Liner Services ex North-Continent and Adriatic.

BREB is led by high quality standards which are practiced consequently in every operational division, focusing on customer service, environmental protection and safety according to

ISO 9001:2008, ISO 14001:2004, OSHAS 18001:2007.
