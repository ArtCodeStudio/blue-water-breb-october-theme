3. Server log files

Every time a page is requested, our web server automatically records data that your browser transmits. This happens whether or not you enter anything on this site:

— your IP address
— date and time of the request
— the page or file requested and the amount of data transferred
— the page you came from, if any
— the browser and operating system you use

We use this data to deliver the site, to keep it stable and secure and to trace technical faults. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: we have a legitimate interest in a website that works and is not abused. The data is not merged with other sources and is not used to identify you personally. Log files are rotated daily and deleted after 14 days at the latest, unless a specific incident requires us to keep them longer as evidence.

4. Cookies

This website sets exactly one cookie, named winter_session. It holds a random session identifier so that the server can recognise consecutive requests from the same browser — this is what makes form submissions and the content management system work at all. It contains no advertising identifier and no profile, and it is deleted when you close your browser at the latest.

Because this cookie is strictly necessary to provide the service you requested, we may store it without your consent under § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG; the legal basis for the associated processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We do not use any cookies that require consent, which is why this site shows no cookie banner.

6. Contacting us by e-mail, telephone or fax

This website has no contact form. If you write, call or fax us using the details published here, we process the data you provide — typically your name, your contact details and the content of your enquiry — in order to answer it.

Where your enquiry relates to a contract or to steps taken before entering into one, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. In all other cases we rely on our legitimate interest in dealing with enquiries addressed to us, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We keep this data until your enquiry has been dealt with and you ask us to delete it, unless statutory retention periods require us to keep it longer.

Please note that e-mail sent over the internet is not encrypted end to end and can in principle be read by third parties. Please do not send confidential information by unencrypted e-mail.

8. Job applications

If you apply to us — by e-mail to bewerbung@breb.de, by post or through our vacancies page — we process the data you send us in order to decide on your application. This may include your contact details, your CV, certificates and any notes we take during the application process.

The legal basis is § 26(1) BDSG in conjunction with Art. 88 GDPR and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, both of which cover the processing needed to decide whether to enter into an employment relationship. Where you give us data voluntarily that goes beyond this, the basis is your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

If we do not offer you a position, we delete your documents six months after we notify you, unless a longer period is needed to defend against legal claims or you have agreed that we may keep them longer. With your consent we include your application in our applicant pool, where it is deleted after two years at the latest. If you are hired, the data is transferred to your personnel file.

11. SSL/TLS encryption

13. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding the data we hold about you:

Access — Art. 15 GDPR: you may ask us at any time, free of charge, what data we process about you, where it came from, whom we disclose it to and how long we keep it.

Rectification — Art. 16 GDPR: you may ask us to correct inaccurate data and to complete incomplete data.

Erasure — Art. 17 GDPR: you may ask us to delete your data, unless we are required or entitled to keep it, for example because of statutory retention periods.

Restriction of processing — Art. 18 GDPR: instead of erasure you may ask us to restrict processing, for instance while a request for rectification is being examined.

Data portability — Art. 20 GDPR: you may ask us to hand over the data you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or to transmit it to another controller.

Objection — Art. 21 GDPR: where we process data on the basis of a legitimate interest, you may object for reasons arising from your particular situation. We will then stop processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.

Withdrawal of consent — Art. 7(3) GDPR: where you have given consent, you may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise any of these rights, an informal message to info@bw-breb.de is enough.
