Bremer Reederei E & B GmbH offers full agency services in all ports at the river Weser, Elbe, North Sea and Baltic Sea. To ensure best service to our customers we opened 2009 a branch office in Cuxhaven. We handle all commodities, e.g. project cargo, as well as all types of vessels in a professional manner. We also take care of your tonnage during docking or repair in local shipyards. Above that Bremer Reederei participated at the Offshore project "Alpha Ventus", where we supervise a special carrier for plant components.

Since 2011 we are the designated agents for RoRo Liner Services of UECC and Flota Suardiaz. Here we arrange liner connections for any rolling stock or breakbulk "made rolling" from our hub Cuxhaven destined to United Kingdom, Baltic, Scandinavia, Spain or North Africa.
