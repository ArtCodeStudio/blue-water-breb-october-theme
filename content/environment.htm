Bremer Reederei E & B GmbH connects great importance to its environmental management. Environmental management is one of our highest corporate priorities. Every year, we continue to enhance maritime environmental protection. Our team puts great effort into the performance of environmental-friendly sea-transportation, focusing on trying to minimize emissions transferring into the maritime, sea and air environments. Three of our vessels have been awarded between 2004-2010 with the UNEP- label (United Nations Envionmental Programme) “Environment- conscious Ship Operation RAL-UZ 110”, called “Blauer Engel”. 


Furthermore, our efforts were awarded with the “price environment corporation: NorthWest 2007” (“preis umwelt unternehmen: Nordwest 2007”) and the special price logistic by the Kieserling trust in 2008. 


Bremer Reederei is also member of “partnership environment corporation” (partnerschaft umwelt unternehmen).
