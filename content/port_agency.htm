Baltic Sea Bridge

In close co-operation with Mukran Port Terminals BREB operates the Container-Line "Baltic Sea Bridge" between Mukran and Baltysk as integrated part of the New Silkroad from China to Europe. Since July 2019, BREB GmbH & Co. KG is a member of OTIF (Intergovernmental Organisation for International Carriage by Rail) and officially approved for the carriage of Containers under a continuous rail waybill (CIM) from Baltysk to Mukran and v.v.. Blue Water BREB offers full port agency service in Mukran.

More information at Baltic Sea Bridge or OTIF (EN).
