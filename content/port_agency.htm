BREB offers Port-Agency in all German Ports and Antwerp-Zeebrugge-range to Shipping-Lines, Charteres and Owners as well as for Offshore Projects. Being a Shipowner ourselfs we have a good understanding of what a Vessel and Owner needs. Running our own Liner-Services we also understand quite well the needs of a Shipping-Line and Charterer.

In the port of Cuxhaven we act as Liner-Agents for UECC and Flota Suardiaz. Appointed by Charterers and Owners we provide services to conventional vessels for loading and discharging operations of Project-cargoes, Generals, Breakbulk and Bulk. We also arrange loading and discharging operations at various terminals for our clients.

Special care is given to vessels while under class-survey and/or repair in local Drydocks.

Our service includes but is not limited to full Crew change arrangements, custom clearance, Cargo Documents, Supply of Bunkers and all Consumables etc.
