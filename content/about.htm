Established in 1951, Bremer Reederei E & B GmbH operates using traditional values such as reliability and punctuality as well as modern technology and flexibility.

We provide our cllients with effective, custom-tailored, and affordable sea transportation. We employ a fleet of the newest highly sophisticated box-shaped open-hatch vessels including highest Ice Class.

Bremer Reederei E & B GmbH is managed by business partner Arne Ehlers. Our team benefits from Captain Ehlers experience on worldwide trade and his great knowledge of the shipping industry. Cpt. Ehlers Curriculum Vitae include the roles as Deep Sea Master, Nautical Supervisor, Terminal Manager and Chartering Manager.

Together with the strength of the company group, we are capable to cover relevant shipping fields, such as Chartering, Sale & Purchase, Brokerage, Warehousing, Stevedoring and Crewing.
